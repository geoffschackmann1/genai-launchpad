## 2. CONFLICT #1 - CRITICAL - seller payment terms do not match the SBA model

This is the headline issue and must be resolved before submission.

| | SBA financial model & Business Plan | Final MIPA (Rev 2.00, 04-17-26) |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $300,000 | $300,000 (match) |
| Structure | **Seller note, 36 months at 6%** | **12 equal monthly installments of $25,000, interest-free** |
| Monthly | $9,126.58/mo | $25,000/mo |
| Year-1 seller cash out | $109,519 | **$300,000** (all 12 installments fall in ~Year 1) |
| First payment | At close | 30 days after diligence-satisfaction notice |

**Why it matters - it breaks Year-1 coverage as modeled:**
- Modeled combined debt service (Year 1): $193,876 (SBA $84,357 + seller $109,519) -> DSCR 1.56x.
- Actual combined obligation (Year 1): SBA $84,357 + seller $300,000 = **$384,357** -> DSCR on
  $302,522 EBITDA is **~0.79x - below the 1.25x SBA floor.**

The entire Business Plan / credit memo / sensitivity analysis assume a 36-month amortizing seller
note. The executed MIPA front-loads the full $300,000 into the first twelve months. An SBA
underwriter running global DSCR on the actual terms will see Year-1 coverage well under 1.0x.

**Options (decision needed - involves the sellers via Jon Greiner, and John Hart):**
1. **Renegotiate the seller payment terms to a 36-month note** (the assumption the model is built
   on). Cleanest for SBA. Sellers gave interest-free 12-month terms; stretching to 36 months
   (with or without modest interest) materially improves coverage. **Recommended.**
2. **Re-run the model on the actual 12-month terms** and show that the $672K working-capital
   reserve funds the $25K/mo from liquidity. Honest, but Year-1 DSCR optics stay poor and a
   conservative lender may decline or require a covenant/holdback.
3. **A hybrid** - partial standby on the seller obligation (if Gleason/Lozano agree), which could
   also let part of it count toward equity under SOP 50 10 8. More complex.

Until this is resolved, do NOT submit the financials as-is - they will not reconcile to the MIPA.

agreement is not advisable** - it would create a conflicting document. The right path is to use

sellers, not a new drafting exercise on our side.
