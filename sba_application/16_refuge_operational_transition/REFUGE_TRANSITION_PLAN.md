## Critical open question - flag to counsel before acting on anything below
The MIPA structures this as a 49%-now / 51%-later ownership transfer specifically to
stay outside the 36-month rule until 1/8/2027 clears. Taking **full operational
control** now, while ownership stays at 49%, is a different question from an
ownership-percentage change - but it still needs healthcare regulatory counsel
sign-off that:
1. It doesn't itself read as a de facto change of ownership/control that CMS or
   Texas HHSC would treat as triggering the 36-month rule early.
2. Adding Geoff (or designated staff) as an Authorized/Delegated Official on the
   CMS-855A is the correct filing type (a Change of Information, not a Change of
   Ownership) for exercising that operational control.
3. Texas HHSC has its own change-of-control or change-of-administrator notification
   separate from CMS, and what triggers it.

Do not file anything with CMS or HHSC until this is confirmed - get it in writing
from counsel before the new session starts executing on the checklist below.
