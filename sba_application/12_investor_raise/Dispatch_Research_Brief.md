# Research Brief: Investor Prospects — Tyler, Texas

## What this is for
I'm raising $500K in promissory notes (part of a $500K–$1M round, $250K already committed) for a hospice startup in Tyler, Texas — Medicare and Medicaid certified license, proven local team, built-in census. Note terms: 10% annual interest, paid quarterly, 3-year maturity with balloon, prepayable anytime, $50K minimum.

This is a **private raise under Reg D 506(b)** — no public advertising, no cold mass outreach. So the goal is NOT a cold-call list. It's a ranked list of people worth pursuing **through warm introductions**, with the most plausible intro path identified for each.

## Two research tracks

### Track 1 — Angel investors and high-net-worth business figures in Tyler / East Texas
Look for, using only public sources (news, LinkedIn public profiles, org websites, filings):
- Angel networks or investor groups active in East Texas
- Tyler-area business owners and sellers of businesses (recent exits)
- Bank board members and directors (community banks in Smith County)
- Family businesses, oil/gas/land families, commercial real estate owners
- Known local philanthropists (hospital foundations, university boards, church-affiliated giving)
- Tyler Chamber of Commerce leadership and Tyler Economic Development Council figures

### Track 2 — Physicians and physician-entrepreneurs in Tyler
- UT Health East Texas and CHRISTUS Trinity Mother Frances medical staff leadership
- Physician-owners: surgery centers, imaging centers, private clinics (e.g., Texas Spine & Joint Hospital founders/partners — Dr. Charles Gordon is already on my radar)
- Smith County Medical Society officers
- Physician entrepreneurs covered in local business news
- Retired physicians with local ties

## Capture for EVERY prospect
| Field | What I need |
|---|---|
| Name | Full name |
| Role / affiliation | Current position, company/practice |
| Why them | 1–2 sentences: evidence of capacity and any healthcare/senior-care interest |
| Warm path | Most plausible introduction route (banking circles, chamber, hospital board, church, medical society, mutual business ties) |
| Est. check capacity | Rough tier: $50K / $100K / $250K+ if inferable |
| Source URL | Working public link for every claim |
| **Referral-risk tag (physicians only)** | See below — this is important |

## Physician referral-risk tag (Anti-Kickback Statute)
Paying investment returns to someone who refers patients to a hospice is legally risky. Tag every physician:
- **LOW** — retired, non-clinical, or specialties that would never refer to hospice (orthopedic surgery, ophthalmology, dermatology, radiology, anesthesia, ENT, sports medicine)
- **HIGH** — anyone who plausibly refers to hospice: oncology, geriatrics, primary care/internal medicine, pulmonology, cardiology, nephrology, neurology, hospitalists, palliative care, nursing home medical directors

HIGH-tag prospects stay on the list but flagged — they need healthcare regulatory counsel sign-off before I can take their money.

## Ranking
Rank the combined list by: (1) strength/plausibility of the warm path, (2) estimated capacity, (3) evidence of interest in healthcare or local investing. LOW referral risk outranks HIGH at equal scores.

## Ground rules
- Public sources only; every entry needs a live source URL
- No contact information scraping beyond what's publicly listed on business/org pages
- Flag anything unverified as unverified — I'd rather have 20 solid names than 60 guesses
- Exclude: current employees of competing hospices, anyone currently in litigation with local health systems (if discoverable), elected officials

## Output format
A ranked table (markdown or CSV) with the capture fields above, split into the two tracks, with a short "top 10 overall" summary at the top and a note on which warm paths appear most frequently (so I know which connectors to prioritize).
